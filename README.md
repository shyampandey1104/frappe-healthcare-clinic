# 🏥 Frappe Healthcare Clinic Management System

[![Python 3.9+](https://img.shields.io/badge/python-3.9+-blue.svg)](https://www.python.org/downloads/)
[![ERPNext](https://img.shields.io/badge/Frappe-Healthcare-blue.svg)](https://frappeframework.com)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

An **Outpatient Clinic & Healthcare OPD Electronic Medical Records (EMR)** module for **Frappe Framework**. Manages patient registrations, clinical vitals history, doctor consultations, e-prescriptions with dosage schedules, and outpatient billing.

---

## ⚡ Highlights
- 🩺 **Patient EMR**: Complete clinical encounter history and vital signs records.
- 💊 **E-Prescriptions**: Structured dosage intervals (`1-0-1`) and duration tracking.
- 💳 **OPD Billing**: Consultation charges and medicine dispense invoices.
- 🧪 **Pytest Suite**: Complete unit tests included.
